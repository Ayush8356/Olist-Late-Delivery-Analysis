Olist E-commerce — Late Delivery to Revenue Associated

SQL project with dashboard design · Base: 96,203 delivered orders, 1 Jan 2017 – 31 Aug 2018 · R$ first, INR scale only at 16.233 INR/BRL (17 Nov 2026, exchange-rates.org)

Note: charts below are previews made from the same results CSVs, not Power BI Desktop screenshots yet. Build the 4 pages on Windows in Power BI Desktop from POWER_BI_BUILD_SPEC.md, then replace these with real screenshots.

Wording: “associated with”, never “lost” or “profitability”. Late orders were already paid. No cost data, so no profit is claimed.

Page 3 — Drivers

Caveat: the attribution split is a heuristic — one day of handover delay is assumed to shift delivery by one day, and it cannot see how the estimate was built.

State story, confirmed against results/06_by_state.csv: RJ has 12,310 orders (12.8% of 96,203) but R$152,004 (24.6%) of late-and-bad revenue. Its late rate is 12.14% vs 4.50% for SP.

Caveat: the seller-controlled bad-review gap (54.4 pp) rests on 53 sellers with 20+ late and 20+ on-time reviewed orders each. Joint regression with seller fixed effects: +22.5 / +60.8 / +69.0 pp for 1–3 / 4–9 / 10+ days late. Source: results/03_controlled_summary.csv, results/03d_regression.csv

Page 4 — Freight burden

Source: results/05_freight_by_category.csv, results/05_freight_by_state.csv

Freight burden = freight as % of basket (price + freight). Not profitability. Highest categories: electronics 22.8%, food_drink 22.7%. Highest states: MA 20.8%, RO 19.8%, SE 19.5%.
